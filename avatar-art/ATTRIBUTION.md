# Avatar art attribution

The `brows/soft.svg` and `brows/raised.svg` shapes are adapted from the Default and RaisedExcited eyebrow components in [Avataaars](https://github.com/fangpenlin/avataaars) by Pablo Stanley and Fang-Pen Lin. They were scaled, positioned, and recolored for Tablemate's 256 × 256 layer catalog. Avataaars is MIT licensed; the full notice is in [AVATAAARS-LICENSE.txt](AVATAAARS-LICENSE.txt).

The rest of the Tablemate avatar layers remain Tablemate artwork. No LPC sprite assets are included.
